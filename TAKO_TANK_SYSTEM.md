# TAKO TANK — THE WHOLE SYSTEM
A living voxel aquarium you share with friends. One tank, one currency (shells), eight levels, animals that remember you. This is the complete map: screens, flow, levels, economy, every system and how they connect. Everything here is read from the game's rules as they are in build v329.

---

## 1. The idea in one paragraph
You start with a free octopus of your own and a small tank. You feed, clean and play; the tank's health decides how fast it grows through eight levels; each level opens animals, decorations and ways to play. Shells come from the day's mystery, requests, visitors, goals and milestones, never from the chores themselves, and you spend them on animals and decorations. The animals have temperaments, relationships, favorite places and memories; they breed, pair for life, grow old. Level 7 prepares a sanctuary; level 8 opens sea turtles, which you raise and release to the ocean, and they come back to visit. Several caretakers can share one tank and see the same living state.

## 2. Screens and flow
**Opening.** A loading screen (logo, a low-poly octopus peeking in). First time: "Welcome to your tank": choose your octopus's color (Coral, Violet, Ocean, Amber, Kelp, Ink), name it, "Bring it home". No tutorial beyond a few first-time one-line tips when a tab is first opened; the tank is the main screen and everything is learned by tapping.

**The tank (home).** Full-screen 3D tank: sand on three levels (front shelf, middle terrace, high back bank with ramps), plants and decorations, far kelp and a reef wall in the haze, caustics, sunbeams, bubbles, the sky's real time of day (morning / afternoon / evening / night from your clock, with the real moon phase and the odd rainy day). Top left: shell pill (your balance). Top right: two rings (gold = level progress, colored = Tank Health) with a notice badge. Tap an animal: its name pill appears with what it is doing and "More info ›" (opens its card). Tap the water or sand: toss a toy, drop food, interact. Hold and drag: the light spot, or "play with" an animal (crabs and the octopus follow your finger). Bottom bar: **Tank · Care · Shop · Crew**.

**Care** (sheet). Two segments: **To do** and **Play**.
- *To do*: what needs you right now, in order (Feed → a list of who is hungry and what they eat; Animal health → a ring per animal and its needs; Tank upkeep → wipe glass, change water, with "next due"), then **Today and coming up** (the day's mystery, requests, decisions waiting for your call, eggs due, pregnancies, birthdays, level ETA), your current goal, today's news.
- *Play*: things to do with the octopus (Hide-and-seek, Puzzle jar, tricks on request), the **Toy box** (ball, bubbles, twist cube, hitching hoop, light spot, each shows who knows it), Share (postcards), "They would love" (wishes).
- **Feed** screen: an animal per card with its food and hunger state (Full / Peckish / Hungry), one tap feeds; food drops where they are. Foods: Flakes (free), Pellets 2, Treats 4; Mysis shrimp for seahorses (free), a crab for the octopus (free, from Feed), Sea greens for turtles 3.
- **Health**: every animal's wellbeing score with the wants it has met and not met (room, cover, food competition, a den, a holdfast, a ledge…), read from the real tank.

**Shop** (sheet). Tabs: **NEW** (what the last level opened and you don't have), Plants, Rocks, Wood, Structures, Special, **Sea life**, Shells (empty shells for hermit crabs, from level 3), Themes, Floor, Backdrop. Each card: the real 3D model, name, price or "Lv N" if locked, tags (NEW, ♥ who wants it, +health, "carries sapphire" for a color carrier, "sanctuary adoption"). Tap a card → preview: what it is, who it helps, what it does to Tank Health (before → after, tried on a copy of your tank), themes it counts toward, Adopt/Buy or "N more shells needed" / "Unlocks at level N" / "After a release". Buying an animal: name it; it arrives after its wait (minutes to hours) in a delivery bag. Buying a decoration: place it with drag/rotate (Place / Cancel / Sell).

**Crew** (sheet). Segments **Friends** (the tank's caretakers, invite code, avatars, who feeds whom, gifts sent/received, notes on the glass), **Your animals** (every animal with its card; the bred-here family gallery by generation), **Journal** (everything that happened, day by day, with your own moments and photos; "Tank history"). Gear → **Settings** (build version, sound, notifications, dev tools).

**Our Tank** (the rings, top right). **Today**: "How your tank grows" explainer the first time, then the level ring and health ring with the next level's prize, the road of levels, the best thing to do now, the turtle savings line (price, how many more shells, shared, habitat ready?, room), thriving-days toward a new look, postcards. **Collection**: the field book of every species and decoration found (N of 72), the Field Guide pages (behaviors you have caught), themes.

**Animal card** (from the tank or Crew). Name (editable), species and kind, traits, sex, stage, days here, wellbeing word and %. Tabs **Overview** (right now: needs met, next growth, play buttons: follow your finger / tricks / puzzle, Trust line for the octopus, Milestones row: came home, made a friend, found a favorite spot, trusts you, learned a trick, all grown up, became a parent; looking after it), **Personality** (temperament and six sliders: curiosity, sociability, boldness, activity, adaptability, bond with you; behaviors seen 0/14), **Stats** (age, days in tank, distance travelled, where it spends its time), **Journal** (its own entries). Pair with…, Sell / find a new home, Release (turtles).

**Other surfaces.** Toasts (short, calm); the coach line (one hint at a time, steps aside when a sheet is up); level-up banner (everyone swims to the glass) and a "New for you" dialog; "While you were away" recap on return; weekly recap card; decision cards ("Your call"); the hatch reveal; postcards; the Field Guide "spotted" card; the bell/notice list.

## 3. Levels (care points; room = animals the tank holds)

## 4. Shells (the one currency)
Start 20. **Income:** the daily mystery 6 (more for a find); a rare visitor +4 every 2–4 days; today's request +3/5/8; a goal +20 and a floor or backdrop; a theme +3; wishes +4…+25 (three fish, six decorations, level 3, happy fish, three kinds, a grown fish, 12 fish, level 6, 25 fish, 20 in the book, the whole book, top level); level-ups +5…+11; a released turtle 100 + up to ~50 for its wellbeing; selling a fish pays a little (bought fish only); discoveries and milestones a few. Care itself pays nothing. Measured: about 9 shells a day casual, 11 regular, 13 engaged, 13–15 for 2–4 caretakers; balances sit at 15–60 because everything earned gets spent.
**Sinks:** animals and decorations (above), empty crab shells 6–22, floors 20–40 and backdrops 25 (or won from goals), pellets/treats/greens, the puzzle jar 3, hide-and-seek 5, a crab treat (old builds).

## 5. Tank Health
Score = 60 % the animals' average wellbeing + 40 % the tank (50 % water, 30 % glass, 20 % space). Bands: Thriving ≥85, Happy ≥75, Struggling ≥55, Unwell. It drives level speed, the health ring, and whether fish spawn (not in a crowded tank). Water fouls over time (slower with a Maxima Clam), the glass films over (slower with a Blue Tang grazing it). Each animal's wellbeing comes from hunger, health, company, cover, room, its specific wants, friends and trouble (bullies, rivals, clashes).

## 6. Animals
**Fish** (clownfish, chromis, gobies, damselfish, cardinalfish, gramma, angelfish, anthias, tang, puffer, mandarin, dragonets). Traits from a pool (Shy, Brave, Curious, Social, Playful, Lazy, Calm, Greedy) decide how they swim, hide, rest, follow friends, come to the glass. Schools school. Stages: baby 3 days, juvenile until day 10, adult; elders soften with age; lifespans 8–14 months, then a memorial. Fish make best friends and rivals, pick favorite places from where they really spend time (habitat affordances: shelter, climb, hold, rest, forage, interest), rest side by side, dart for cover from big fish, go and look at new decorations.
**Breeding and genetics.** Pair two grown fish of one kind ("Pair with…"); eggs are laid and guarded; a hatching waits for you to watch it; babies carry color genes; rare morphs (Golden, Ghost, Sapphire, Ember) come from carriers (the shop's daily special sometimes "carries" one). How you raise a young one shapes its temperament (pet often → Brave, play ball → Playful, light/bubbles → Curious, feed daily → Calm). Bred-here gallery by generation; send a bred baby to a friend's tank; a weekly rare-color board across tanks.
**Octopus** (your first animal; a second at level 6). Four real kinds (Common, Day, Two-Spot, Mimic) and six temperaments (explorer, homebody, show-off, watcher, collector, trickster) that decide what it does when nothing is going on: impressions (flatfish, lionfish, sea snake, jellyfish, kelp), burying, dashing, following a fish, grabbing and carrying things, standing tall at a rival, squirting at the glass, a tour of the tank. It needs a den (coconut or pot; it carries a coconut home and flips it, lifts the lid), hoards shells and keepsakes, learns the tank (favorites, habits by time of day), changes color to blend with what it sits on, eats crabs (a leg at a time), and builds **trust** with each caretaker (hides → watches you → comes to the glass → takes a crab from your hand). Play: tricks on request (level 3), the puzzle jar, hide-and-seek (it learns your hiding), follow your finger, the twist cube. **Adventures** (level 3 + a week): it pushes at the lid; you decide (weigh it down / a puzzle / let it explore); it escapes and returns with keepsakes and clues; postcards. Octopus pairs (a female and a male with dens) become partners after 3 days, eggs after ~5, hatch after ~5 more (the "An octopus family" goal).
**Seahorses** (level 4). Four kinds, pair only with their own kind, for life, after two days together; morning greeting dance; the father carries the young 6–9 days (brood pouch), babies in the nursery 5 days with their own personalities (explorer, plant-lover, social); they hold on to plants and sea fans with their tails, hunt mysis from the stem, snap at plankton, watch new things with one eye, rest at night on a favorite holdfast, blend toward their plant's color. They tire in busy water; damselfish stress them; puffers nip them.
**Hermit crabs** (levels 3, 4, 6). Three species, six natures (bold, timid, explorer, homebody…), they grow and need bigger shells: empty shells go in the tank and a crab moves house when one fits (a whole swap animation); they pick over the sand, climb rocks and wood, play with toys, follow your finger, dig up an ancient shell. The octopus hunts them for food (a crab from Feed drops in for it).
**Sea turtles** (level 8). Green, Loggerhead, Kemp's Ridley, Hawksbill (after a release): a rescued hatchling arrives, breathes at the surface, grazes sea grass, sleeps under a ledge, counts down on its card; after 14 days grown it can be **released** (pays 100+, leaves a scute keepsake, a postcard, and it comes back to visit now and then). Each species has its own wants: room (the tank ≤60 % full; a turtle counts as four small fish), a ledge or two rocks, sea grass or two plants (green), sponges/live rock (hawksbill), rubble/shells (loggerhead), open sand and company (Kemp's), clean water. The Leatherback only ever passes by.
**Visitors.** Rare fish (Ghost Dragonet, Golden Angelfish, Rose Goby) drop in every 2–4 days (greet them: +4; themes lure their own), released turtles return, a starfish creeps about.

## 7. Decorations, habitat and themes
Every piece has affordances the animals use (plants to hold and hide, rocks to climb and sleep under, dens, stations, ledges, rubble to forage). Placing one shows its real effect: Tank Health before → after, whose want it meets, which theme it advances. **Four reef themes**, each needing a count and a key piece: Kelp Forest (4 of kelp/grass/seaweed/sea whip incl. Giant Kelp, level 3), Coral Garden (4 of anemone/gorgonian/sea fan/coralline/brain/table/spire incl. a Sea Fan or Table Coral, level 4), Sunken Ruins (3 of pot/coconut/pillar/arch/torii/bridge/skull/anchor/chest incl. a chest, anchor, arch, bridge or torii, level 6), Lantern Cove (2 of lantern/crystal/lighthouse/bubbler incl. a Glow Crystal or the Lighthouse, level 7). A finished theme pays +3 once, lifts every fish a little and brings its visitor. Floors (Sand, Pearl, Gravel, Black sand, Pink coral) and backdrops (Candy, Lagoon, Sunset, Mint) change the look; thriving days earn new looks too.

## 8. The daily loop
Open the app → "While you were away" (what happened, who did what) → the coach's one best thing to do → **the mystery**: one glowing thing in the sand a day (tracks, bubbles, a glint); tap it for +6 and a line, some carry into tomorrow → feed who is hungry, wipe or change water when due → **today's request** (+3/5/8) → say hello to a visitor → play (toys, tricks, finger play) → check decisions and eggs → shop or save. Every few days: a **decision** ("Your call": the octopus wants out; who gets the last den; an unwell fish…) with real outcomes; **goals** (long aims with steps and hints: Raise a family, Three temperaments, Field naturalist, An octopus family, A peaceful tank, Three generations, Tank detective, rare-color goals, A turtle sanctuary; +20 and a floor/backdrop; the lighthouse at three); **wishes** (the tank's own list); **chapters** mark landmarks once; a **weekly recap**; **postcards** from released turtles and adventurers; the **Field Guide** fills the first time someone sees a behavior (camouflage, ink, squirt, the impressions, the ball…). Notifications are ranked and capped per day; cliffhangers ("something is hatching tonight") bring you back.

## 9. Sharing a tank (multiplayer)
One shared tank per group, joined by code. Everyone sees the same state: the server owns the simulation clock, the day counter, the shells, the animals; one phone leads the live animation and the others follow its snapshots (positions, poses, seahorse states, octopus pose) so two phones side by side match. Each caretaker has their own octopus and their own feeding duties ("Inky is fed by Blair"); ownership decides who may sell or release. Trust, greetings and "the octopus knows you" are per caretaker. Gifts: send a bred baby to a friend's tank; surprise gifts arrive wrapped. Notes on the glass. Actions are idempotent on the server, so a double tap, a reload or two phones can't pay or buy twice; the sanctuary goal and the first-turtle adoption are once per tank. Lossless rebuild when two phones hold different copies.

## 10. Time, sky and the world
Morning / afternoon / evening / night follow the phone's clock (lighting, shafts, a moon with real phases, bioluminescent plankton at new moon, rainy days). Animals have night routines (seahorses roost, the octopus sleeps in its den and dreams in color, fish rest). The tank keeps living while the app is closed (the server advances it; offline time is capped so nothing dies of neglect in a day: unwell and recovering, never a sudden death; only old age takes an animal).

## 11. Graphics
Pixel/voxel identity throughout: every animal is a voxel model with a wet sheen, mottling and baked shading; three-level sand with ripples, contact shadows and caustics; sunbeams, bubbles with glints, soft shadow discs under animals; layered far kelp and a reef wall fading into depth fog; real night; a filmic grade with a light 15-bit dither; depth of field eased on close-ups. Menus share one cool glass material. Logo: voxel octopus perched on "TAKO TANK".

## 12. After level 8 (the long game)
No seasons, no resets, no second tank. Points keep banking. Life goes on through: raising and releasing turtles (every species, each with its habitat), their return visits; octopus relationships, families and adventures; seahorse pairs and generations; fish breeding, rare colors and three-generation lines; hermit crabs growing into bigger shells; redesigning the habitat and watching who uses what; animal memories, favorites and habits; the Field Guide; goals, visitors and decisions; the people you share the tank with.
