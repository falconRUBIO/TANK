OUR TANK · PRIVACY

A small shared aquarium for four friends. No ads, no purchases, no tracking by other companies.

WHAT IS STORED

  • The name and avatar you chose.
  • Your tank: fish, decorations, shells, the journal, chat messages and notes you write to your friends.
  • If you turn notifications on: the address your phone gives the browser to send you notifications.
  • Your sign-in is kept on your phone in the browser's storage and one first-party cookie, so a crash does not lose your tank; it is never shared. A recovery key, stored only as a one-way hash. Nobody, including the developer, can read it back.
  • Anonymous usage counts (for example "a fish was fed") tied to a random id, not to your name, so the developer can see whether the game is working. They contain no message text.

WHAT IS NOT

  • No email, phone number, location or contacts.
  • No advertising, no analytics from other companies.

WHO SEES WHAT

The two other people in your tank see your name, avatar, chat and what you do in the tank. Nobody else can see your tank unless you give them its code.

YOUR CONTROLS

  • Download a backup of your tank at any time (Settings). It contains no account ids.
  • Delete my data (Settings) removes you and your account. If you are the last player, the tank and its history are deleted too. This cannot be undone.
